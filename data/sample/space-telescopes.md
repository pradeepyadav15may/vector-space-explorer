# Space Telescopes: Hubble and James Webb

The Hubble Space Telescope launched in April 1990 and orbits about 540 kilometres above Earth. Its images changed astronomy by revealing galaxies, nebulae and the expansion history of the universe with unprecedented clarity.

The James Webb Space Telescope launched on 25 December 2021. It observes mainly in infrared, which lets it see the earliest galaxies whose light has been stretched by the expansion of the universe. Its 6.5-metre segmented mirror is coated in gold to reflect infrared light.

Webb sits about 1.5 million kilometres from Earth, at the second Sun-Earth Lagrange point. At that distance the Sun, Earth and Moon can stay behind a single sunshield, keeping the instruments very cold.
