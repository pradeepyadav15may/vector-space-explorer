# Blue Whales: The Largest Animals Known

The blue whale is the largest animal known to have ever lived. Adults can reach about 30 metres in length and weigh more than 150 tonnes, which is heavier than most dinosaurs.

Blue whales are filter feeders. They take in huge mouthfuls of seawater and push it out through baleen plates, trapping krill, which can number in the millions in a single feeding area. A large whale may eat several tonnes of krill in a day during the summer feeding season.

Their calls are low-frequency sounds that can travel hundreds of kilometres through the ocean. Researchers use recordings of these calls to track populations and to study how noise pollution affects animals that rely on sound.
