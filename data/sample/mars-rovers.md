# Mars Rovers: Curiosity and Perseverance

Curiosity landed in Gale Crater in August 2012. Its instruments have shown that the crater once held a lake and that the environment could have supported microbial life, though no evidence of life has been confirmed.

Perseverance landed in Jezero Crater on 18 February 2021. Jezero was once a lake fed by a river delta, which makes its sediments a promising place to look for signs of ancient life. The rover also collects rock cores for a future sample return mission.

Perseverance carried the Ingenuity helicopter, which made the first powered, controlled flight on another planet on 19 April 2021. Radio signals take between four and twenty-four minutes to travel between Earth and Mars, so rovers operate with a large degree of autonomy.
